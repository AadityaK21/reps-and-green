FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I talk to Sanya?

Open the Coach tab in the app and send a message. It goes straight to her inbox, and she replies there.

Is the app free?

Yes. Your workout plan, meal tracking, chat and class booking are free. Premium adds personalised plans and priority replies.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. Message Sanya in the app and she will update your workouts or calories.

Is my payment safe?

Payments go through Razorpay. Reps & Greens never sees or stores your card details.

Can I use it on my laptop?

Yes. Log in at this website on any phone or laptop. Your data stays in sync.
