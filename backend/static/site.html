Install Merova Fit on your phone for a full-screen app with its own icon, notifications from your coach, and one tap to log your day.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I talk to my coach?

Open the Coach tab in the app and send a message. It goes straight to your coach, who replies there.

Is the app free?

Yes. Your workout plan, meal tracking, chat and class booking are free. Premium adds personalised plans and priority replies.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. Message your coach in the app and they will update your workouts or calories.

Is my payment safe?

Payments go through Razorpay. Merova Fit never sees or stores your card details.

Can I use it on my laptop?

Yes. Log in at this website on any phone or laptop. Your data stays in sync.
