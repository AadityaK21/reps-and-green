Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

We want you to be happy with Reps & Greens. Here is how cancellations, refunds and returns work.

Subscriptions and coaching plans

  • Cancel anytime: in the app, open Membership plan and choose Cancel subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for, and you won't be charged again.
  • Within 7 days of your first payment: if coaching isn't right for you, email us within 7 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full.
  • After 7 days: payments for a period that has already started are not refunded, except when the law requires it or we were unable to provide the service.
  • Charged by mistake (for example, a double payment): we refund the extra amount in full.

Shop products

  • Damaged, wrong or missing items: tell us within 7 days of delivery, with a photo, and we will replace the item or refund it.
  • Unopened items: you can return them within 7 days of delivery for a refund. Return shipping is paid by you unless the item was damaged or wrong.
  • Opened food and supplements can't be returned for hygiene and safety reasons, unless they arrived damaged or expired.
  • Cancelling an order: you can cancel before it ships for a full refund.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take a few extra days to show it.

How to ask

Email chameleon.aaditya@gmail.com with your account email and order details, or message your coach in the app. We reply within 2 working days.
